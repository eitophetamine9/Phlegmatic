1. PROJECT SUMMARY & VISION
================================================================================
"Phlegmatic" is a dark fantasy, top-down, high-tempo medieval action RPG set in the
shattered Land of Tydeshire. The game blends deliberate precision combat (tight parry
windows, directional dodging with invulnerability frames, stamina management, and
weapon choreography) with medieval physiological lore centered on the Four Humors
(Sanguine, Choleric, Melancholic, and Phlegmatic).

Key Aesthetic & Technical Pillars:
- Rich procedural vector rendering combined with custom illustration drops.
- Procedural acoustic audio synthesis (SoundFX.cpp) creating authentic cathedral bells,
  metallic parry rings, heavy armor impacts, and portcullis rumbles without bulky WAV dependencies.
- Unified medieval typography engine with custom font atlas, codepoint mapping, and
  special character sanitization.
- Dual-page medieval grimoire lore journal with real-time 3D page curl animation,
  illuminated drop caps, runic seals, and location discovery clues.
- Multi-slot binary/JSON save architecture with timestamped metadata and autosave.
- Zero-external-dependency standalone executable deployment.

4. CURRENT FEATURES & SYSTEMS (VERSION 0.0.1b)
================================================================================
1. Four Humors Alignment & Dynamic Auras:
   - Enemies and player can be aligned with one of four classical bodily humors:
     - Sanguine (Air / Blood): High speed, bleed over time, red chromatic trail.
     - Choleric (Fire / Yellow Bile): High damage, armor shredding, amber flame aura.
     - Melancholic (Earth / Black Bile): High armor, heavy poise, earthen particle ground cracks.
     - Phlegmatic (Water / Phlegm): Calm fortitude, projectile deflection, glacial cyan pulse.
   - Quenching & Shattering: Exploiting opposing humors triggers bonus damage and poise breaks.

2. Phlegmatic Composure System:
   - Dynamic 0-100 Composure Meter positioned beneath the Stamina bar.
   - Passive Tiers:
     - 0-29 (Agitated): Base stamina regen, standard parry window (0.15s).
     - 30-59 (Steady): +15% stamina regeneration rate.
     - 60-89 (Resolute): +25% stamina regen, +10% physical defense, +0.03s parry window.
     - 90-100 (Phlegmatic): +40% stamina regen, +20% defense, +0.05s parry window.
   - Phlegmatic Trance (`SPACE` / `[F]` key at 100 Composure):
     - 5.0 seconds of complete invulnerability and zero stamina consumption.
     - Radial deflection wave pushes back foes and reflects incoming missiles.

3. Combat Juice & Kinetic Impact:
   - Hit-Stop Micro-Pause: 0.06s freeze on critical hits, heavy staggers, and parries.
   - Directional Camera Shake: Directional screen impulse aligned with strike angle,
     damped using polynomial trauma decay.

4. Will Stat & Combat Mathematics:
   - Enemy Will Stat: Dictates poise resistance against staggers and stunlocks.
   - Stagger Formula: Duration = BaseStagger * (100 / (100 + EnemyWill)).
   - Anti-Stunlock Buffer: Enemies gain temporary stagger immunity after being stunned twice in 3 seconds.
   - Health Potions: Player carries up to 3 elixirs, healing +45 HP (`1` or `H` key), 7.0s cooldown.

5. Shingles Currency & Ground Loot Physics:
   - Physical Loot Drops: Slaying enemies drops golden Shingles coins with arc trajectories,
     gravity, and floor bounce physics.
   - Magnetic Pickup: Coins smoothly glide toward player when within 60px proximity.
   - Persistent Currency Counter rendered in top-right HUD.

6. Merchant & Crafting Progression:
   - Blacksmith Varek (Forge Interior):
     - Weapon upgrade system with escalating Shingles cost.
     - Increases light/heavy base damage and armor penetration.
   - Tavernkeep Eldon (Hearthside Tavern):
     - Sells health elixirs, hearty meals for temporary max health buffs, and local rumors.
   - Dedicated Environments: Separate MapEnvironment states for Blacksmith Forge and Tavern.

7. World Expansion & Progression Gating:
   - Royal Keep: Expanded to 1200x1100 interior with ornate tiled floors, royal banners, and stained glass.
   - King Kreptu: Centered upon royal throne dais with Royal Guard escort; story progression is strictly
     gated through dialogue with the King.
   - Knight Lenon: Defeat in duel allows player to speak with him again; duel initiates only after
     the conversational dialogue window is closed.
   - Universal Proximity Prompts: World interaction labels remain completely hidden until within range.
   - Tutorial Cleanup: Slaying Zil permanently suppresses enemy respawns in the tutorial ruins.

8. Animated Grimoire / Lore Journal:
   - Two-page open medieval leather-bound book aesthetic.
   - Aged parchment paper texture, deckle edges, ribbing shadows, and crimson silk ribbon bookmark.
   - 3D Animated Page-Flip Effect: Sinusoidal perspective compression (`cosf(angle)`), moving drop
     shadows, and edge highlights when turning pages (`[A]` / `[D]` keys or click buttons).
   - Recovery Compass Clues: Every one of the 10 lore fragments features explicit in-game location
     clues (e.g., Ruined Castle ramparts, Crossroads shrine, Zyphden square, Keep antechamber).
   - Illuminated drop caps, authentic title typography, and runic seal plaques on shrouded pages.

9. Zyphden Transition System:
   - Responsive screen-space hit testing ensuring clicks register at all resolutions (1080p, 1440p, 4K).
   - Multi-key input support: `SPACE`, `ENTER`, `E`, and mouse click advance through gates.
   - High-resolution 16:9 limestone rampart artwork installed at `assets/zyphden_transition.png`.

10. 3-Slot Save Architecture:
    - Independent save slots (`save_slot_1.json`, `save_slot_2.json`, `save_slot_3.json`).
    - Tracks play time, chapter, level, health, inventory, shingles, composure, and collected lore.
    - Title Screen Record Select modal displays timestamps, current realm, and delete/load options.

8. IMPORTANT CONVENTIONS & GOTCHAS FOR FUTURE MAINTAINERS
================================================================================
1. Zero Emojis Constraint:
   - Never use emojis anywhere in the codebase, comments, git commit messages, documentation, or logs.
   - Use clean, professional textual formatting and standard ASCII / Unicode symbols only where supported.

2. Screen Dimensions vs Window Scaling:
   - Always query `GetScreenWidth()` and `GetScreenHeight()` for modal overlays, transition screens,
     and HUD bounding boxes rather than relying solely on static `Config::WINDOW_WIDTH` (1280) and
     `Config::WINDOW_HEIGHT` (720).
   - When updating hit-tests for buttons, verify that the coordinates match the render pass precisely.

3. Typography Engine (`FontManager`):
   - Never use raw `DrawText()` for body paragraphs or dialogue. Use `FontManager::DrawText()` or
     `FontManager::DrawTextWrapped()` to ensure proper kerning, line heights, and glyph substitution.

4. Binary Distributions:
   - Always compile using `cmd.exe /c build.bat`. Ensure both `phlegmatic.exe` (root) and
     `bin/phlegmatic.exe` are updated in tandem.
